Your Privacy Summary: JAGY is designed with a strict local-first privacy architecture. All your OAuth credentials, channel configurations, API keys, downloaded videos, and rendered Shorts remain saved strictly on your local machine. We do not collect, transmit, or monetize any of your personal data.

01 Overview & Philosophy

At JAGY ("we", "our", or "the Project"), accessible at jagy.in and through the JAGY Desktop Application, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when you use the JAGY desktop application, local server daemon, and web interface.

Because JAGY operates directly on your hardware as self-hosted local software, you maintain complete ownership and control over your digital footprint, credentials, and video assets at all times.

02 100% Local Data Storage

When you run JAGY on your computer, all configuration files, tokens, and temporary files are created locally inside your application directory:

  • YouTube OAuth Tokens (token.json): Stored locally in your system's project folder to maintain authorization with your YouTube channel.
  • Application Configurations (config.yaml): Channel target lists, upload schedules, video quality options, and optional custom API keys are saved locally.
  • Video Media & Subtitles: Raw downloaded videos, audio transcripts, clipped segments, and exported MP4 vertical Shorts reside solely on your local hard drive.

None of these local files are ever uploaded or transmitted to any central JAGY server.

03 YouTube API Services Disclosure

JAGY uses YouTube API Services to allow users to authenticate their YouTube accounts and upload generated video Shorts directly to their channels.

By using JAGY's YouTube upload features, you acknowledge and agree to be bound by:

  • YouTube Terms of Service
  • Google Privacy Policy
⚠️
YouTube Data Handling: JAGY requests Google OAuth permissions (specifically https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload) strictly to enable automated video publishing initiated by you. JAGY does not access your private videos, reading history, or personal profile data beyond what is necessary to upload your authorized Shorts.

04 AI Processing & Gemini API

To identify viral moments and analyze transcripts, JAGY communicates with Google Gemini API services.

During moment extraction:

  • Only video transcripts, dialogue text, and timing metadata are transmitted to the Gemini API endpoint.
  • No personally identifiable information (PII) or user credentials are sent to AI processing services.
  • Users may choose to use JAGY's bundled built-in API keys or supply their own custom Google AI Studio key.

05 Third-Party Integrations

If you enable optional multi-platform syndication (such as Instagram Reels, X/Twitter, or Reddit), JAGY uses official platform APIs or user-configured webhook tokens. Data transmitted to these platforms is strictly governed by their respective privacy policies:

  • Instagram / Meta Graph API: Subject to Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • X (Twitter) API v2: Subject to X's Privacy Policy.
  • Reddit API: Subject to Reddit's Data and Privacy Terms.
  • Pexels API: Used for optional royalty-free background B-roll media.

06 Zero Telemetry & Cookies

We believe software should not track you.

  • No Cookies: The JAGY public website (jagy.in) does not place tracking cookies or advertising pixels on your browser.
  • No Analytics Telemetry: The JAGY Desktop software contains zero phone-home tracking, usage analytics, or background telemetry.
  • No Data Sales: We do not sell, trade, or monetize your usage data, video content, or channel activity.

07 User Revocation & Control

You retain full authority to revoke JAGY's access to your YouTube channel or API services at any time:

  • Local Token Revocation: Delete the config/token.json file on your computer to immediately sign out.
  • Google Account Permissions: Revoke JAGY's OAuth access anytime via the Google Security Settings Page.
  • Complete Data Removal: Simply delete the JAGY application folder to remove all local logs, configurations, and video assets permanently.
